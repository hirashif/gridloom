FAQ

Fair questions.

Is it really my key? What do you see?+

Yes. Keys are stored in your browser's localStorage and sent only to the provider you're calling. Gridloom has no backend to see anything — open the network tab and watch: zero requests to us.

What does it actually cost to run?+

Whatever the providers charge — typically $0.003–$0.04 per image. A 12-image comparison grid usually lands between 10 and 50 cents. Gridloom quotes the exact number before every run.

Why not just Midjourney or ChatGPT?+

They give you one model, one image at a time, and no memory of how you got there. Gridloom is about repeatability: compare models side by side, pin seeds, keep every parameter, and re-run the winning setup forever. Also: no $20–60/month.

What happens if Gridloom disappears?+

The app keeps working — it's a static page talking to providers with your keys. Your library lives in your browser and exports to a plain zip anytime. There's no server to shut down and lose your data on.

Which models are supported?+

Seven models across three providers at launch: fal.ai (FLUX schnell, dev and 1.1 pro, Fast SDXL, Recraft v3), Google Gemini (Nano Banana), and OpenAI (GPT Image 1). The registry updates itself — new models appear in Gridloom without waiting for an app release, free on every tier.

Do I need keys for all three providers?+

No — one is enough to start. The comparison grid simply shows the models your keys can reach. Most people start with fal.ai (five of the seven launch models on one key, from $0.003/image).

Is there a free trial?+

Right now everything is free — we're in early access. After launch, single-model generation stays free forever and the trial includes 5 free comparison runs; a license unlocks unlimited grids.

What's the refund policy?+

14 days, no questions asked, via LemonSqueezy. If Gridloom doesn't earn its price in two weeks of real work, you shouldn't pay for it.
